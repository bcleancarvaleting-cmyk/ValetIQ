Car Detailing in Wye — FAQs

What is the difference between a valet and a detail?

A valet cleans your car. A detail goes further — decontaminating and machine polishing the paint to remove swirls and defects, then protecting it, usually with a ceramic coating.

How long does paint correction take?

An enhancement detail takes one day; a two-stage correction with interior is usually two days. We will confirm timings when you book.

Can you detail my car at home in Wye?

Yes. We are fully mobile and bring our own water — we just need a power socket nearby. For the best results we ask for a driveway or covered space for longer details.

Will polishing remove all scratches?

Most swirls and light scratches can be removed or greatly reduced. Deep scratches through the clear coat may need touch-up — we will always tell you honestly beforehand.
