Car Valeting in Coxheath — FAQs

How much does a car valet cost in Coxheath?

A maintenance valet starts from £80 and a full valet from £100 for a small car. Larger vehicles and heavily soiled interiors cost a little more — you will see the exact price when you book online.

Do I need to provide water or electricity?

We bring our own water, so you do not need a tap. We just need access to a power socket within reach of the car — on a driveway, at your workplace or anywhere in Coxheath with a socket nearby.

How long does a full valet take?

Around three hours for a typical car. Intensive deep cleans for very dirty or pet-hair-heavy cars can take up to five hours.

Can you remove pet hair and bad smells?

Yes — we offer specialist pet hair removal and ozone odour treatment as add-ons to any valet.
