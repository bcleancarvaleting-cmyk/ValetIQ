Ceramic Coating in Snodland — FAQs

How much is a ceramic coating in Snodland?

Our New Car Protection Package starts from £330, and an enhancement detail with a 2-year ceramic coating from £500. The price depends on the size and condition of your car.

How long does a ceramic coating last?

Our coatings come with a warranty of up to several years, provided the car is maintained with safe washing and an annual inspection.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes — every coating comes with a B Clean Valeting certificate of protection showing your vehicle, product, layers and warranty date, with a QR code to verify it.

Can I wash my car after it has been coated?

Wait 7 days, then use a pH-neutral shampoo and the two-bucket method. Avoid automatic car washes. We also offer maintenance washes for coated cars.
